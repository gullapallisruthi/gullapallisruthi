Academic Statement

My drive for medicine stems directly from my family. Watching my relatives in India navigate complex medical challenges showed me firsthand how much healthcare impacts a family’s entire life. Those experiences lit a fire in me. I don’t just want to study medicine; I want to provide compassionate, holistic care that truly makes a difference.

Alongside my medical focus, I am deeply fascinated by psychology. I believe that understanding how people think and feel is the crucial first step to helping them become better versions of themselves. This ties perfectly into how I view healthcare: it’s not just about treating a symptom, but treating the whole person.

Through the Active Learning Institute, I’ve refined how I learn—moving away from memorization and toward deeply understanding and communicating complex ideas. I apply this same philosophy to my community work. As the founder of Sparked Initiative, I actively build educational and medical awareness programs because I believe knowledge should be shared, not gatekept.

This portfolio serves as a living document of my projects, research, and milestones as I prepare for a career where science meets human connection.

Initiative

Problems I Noticed & Action Taken

Identifying challenges in my community and taking proactive steps to solve them.

The Problem

Lack of Medical Awareness Among Peers

Many students are interested in medicine but lack understanding of the diverse career paths and public health issues affecting our community.

What I Did

I am organizing a medical-focused club at school to host guest speakers, discuss public health topics, and connect students with shadowing opportunities.

The Problem

Ineffective Note-Taking Strategies

I noticed that many peers, including myself, struggled to retain information because we were just copying slides rather than synthesizing information.

What I Did

I developed a disciplined note-taking system during my coaching sessions and now host events to teach these effective study skills to younger students.

The Problem

Lack of Basic First Aid Knowledge in Kids

I noticed that young children often lack an understanding of basic medicine and CPR, which are critical skills during life-threatening situations.

What I Did

I organized an interactive event where children practiced with CPR dummies and learned the fundamentals of medicine in a hands-on environment.

The Problem

Support for Vulnerable Children

My peers and I noticed that local children's hospitals and shelters for homeless youth were in need of additional resources, funding, and community support.

What We Did

Co-founded "Magic Makers," a volunteer group that organizes fundraisers like hot chocolate stands. We donate proceeds to children's hospitals while earning service hours.

Philosophy

My Leadership Style

Reflecting on the core values that drive my actions and how I strive to support those around me.

What kind of leader am I?

I am a leader who notices when something isn’t working and takes initiative to improve it. I believe leadership is about being dependable, prepared, and willing to step up even when it’s uncomfortable. I don’t lead to stand out, but to make things clearer and more organized for everyone involved.

How do I help others succeed?

I help others succeed by making things feel less overwhelming. I break down information, share what I’ve learned, and create structure so people feel more confident and supported. I try to be patient and approachable, because I know learning is easier when people don’t feel judged.

What values guide my decisions?

The values that guide my decisions are growth, empathy, and accountability. I believe growth comes from effort and learning from mistakes. Empathy helps me understand others and lead with care. Accountability keeps me responsible for my actions and commitments, even when things are challenging.
